Good to know

  • Hold Shift while drawing shapes for squares, circles and snapped angles.
  • With Select, double-click text or a sticky to edit it; double-click empty board for a quick text box.
  • Every object resizes — drag any corner handle of the selection box.
  • Ctrl/⌘ + scroll zooms · plain scroll pans · right-drag pans · pinch with two fingers on touch.
  • Drop an image file anywhere, or paste one with Ctrl/⌘ + V.
  • Your board autosaves in this browser and is restored on reload (Ctrl+S saves immediately).
